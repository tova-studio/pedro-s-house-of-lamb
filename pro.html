Our Lamb

An iconic whole lamb shoulder, slow baked to perfection

Everything at Pedro's House of Lamb starts with one dish: our whole lamb shoulder, made with only the very best New Zealand lamb.

We slow bake it with rosemary & garlic until it's just right, then serve it with a side of scalloped potatoes. It's roast lamb you can take home and share, without spending the afternoon in the kitchen.

  • The very best New Zealand lamb. A whole shoulder, ready to share.
  • Rosemary & garlic. Slow baked to perfection.
  • Scalloped potatoes. Served on the side.

Good to Know

Your questions, answered

Where can I buy Pedro's roast lamb in Christchurch?

We have two Christchurch stores: the original container on Papanui Road in Merivale and our second store at 290 Colombo St in Sydenham. You can also find us at One NZ Stadium on match and event days, or pick up Pedro's Lamb from New World Halswell and Northwood.

How do I place an order?

For orders, call 0800 NZ LAMB (0800 69 5262). You can also call our Colombo Street store on 03 332 9941 or email colombo@pedros.co.nz.

What comes with the whole lamb shoulder?

Our iconic whole lamb shoulder is made with only the very best New Zealand lamb. We slow bake it with rosemary & garlic and serve it with a side of scalloped potatoes.

Can I give Pedro's as a gift?

Yes, and our gift vouchers are always a hit. Digital vouchers are available online, or you can buy physical vouchers in store.

Is there a Pedro's in Queenstown?

Yes. Many visitors put our Queenstown shop on their must-do list, and it's conveniently located with parking.

What can I get at One NZ Stadium?

On match and event days, try our delicious lamb on chips or Pedritos, our lamb burritos.
